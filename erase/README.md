# erase: get your info off people search sites

your name, home address, phone number and relatives are probably listed on these sites, free for anyone to look up. every one of them has an opt out page. it takes an afternoon.

## the big ones (free opt out links)

| site | opt out page |
|---|---|
| spokeo | https://www.spokeo.com/optout |
| beenverified | https://www.beenverified.com/app/optout/search |
| whitepages | https://www.whitepages.com/suppression-requests |
| intelius, us search, instant checkmate, truthfinder | https://suppression.peopleconnect.us/login |
| fastpeoplesearch | https://www.fastpeoplesearch.com/removal |
| nuwber | https://nuwber.com/removal/link |
| radaris | https://radaris.com/control/privacy |
| thatsthem | https://thatsthem.com/optout |
| familytreenow | https://www.familytreenow.com/optout |
| mylife | https://www.mylife.com/ccpa/index.pubview |
| checkpeople | https://checkpeople.com/privacy-rights |

more opt out links at https://simpleoptout.com, a free directory.

google also removes search results with your personal info:
https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/9673730

tip: most of these want the url of your listing, so search your name on the site first, open your profile, copy the link, then hit the opt out page.

## the part everyone skips

they relist you. new data comes in and your profile comes back.

1. set a monthly reminder on your phone: "recheck people search sites"
2. when it fires, google your name + city and redo any opt out that came back
3. paste this when you recheck:

```
i opted out of people search sites last month. help me check if my listings came back. my name is [name], i'm in [city, state]. what should i search and which sites should i recheck first?
```
