# edit: edit video by typing

hyperframes studio is a free app from heygen. you drop in a clip, type what you want, and it builds the edit.

heads up: this one needs a laptop or desktop (mac, windows or linux). it doesn't work on a phone.

- download the free app: https://hyperframes.studio
- help page: https://hyperframes.heygen.com/studio-app

## how to use this

1. on your laptop, open https://hyperframes.studio and hit the download button for your computer. install it like any other app.
2. open it and sign in with a free heygen account (it walks you through it).
3. the first time you ask for a video, it asks you to "connect an agent". that's the ai that does the editing. pick **opencode** if you want it free (no sign in). if you already pay for chatgpt or claude, you can pick codex or claude code and sign in with that account instead.
4. in the "make something" box, tap + and add your clip.
5. paste the box below (fill in the [brackets] first) and hit go. it shows you stills of the plan first, say "build the video" when it looks right.
6. when it's done, export it and it lands in your downloads folder.

it can take a while, sometimes 20 to 40 minutes for the first one, so start it and go do something else. the free plan includes a few cloud exports and ai clips a month, everything else is free. you never need the $25 pro plan for this.

## the starter prompt

```
You are my video editor. Turn my raw clip into a finished short video, not a list of editing tips.

MY INFO:
- My clip: [what's in it, how long]
- Where it's going: [tiktok / reels / shorts]
- Style: [e.g. fast cuts, chill, heavy captions]
- Text and captions: [e.g. word-by-word captions, title card saying "X"]

DO THIS:
1. Cut it vertical 9:16 and trim the dead air at the start.
2. Add word-by-word captions that are easy to read and don't cover the important part.
3. Add a 2-second title card at the top that says [my text].
4. Keep the final edit under [30] seconds.

RULES: Only use what's in my clip, no stock footage unless i ask. Don't change how i look or sound.

OUTPUT: the finished edit plus a list of every change you made.

When done, ask me what to change for version 2.
```

if it gets something wrong, just type the fix like "captions bigger" or "cut the first 3 seconds" and send.
