# sell: find out what your dorm junk is worth

your stuff sells for what people actually paid, not what sellers are asking. these prompts price it from real sold listings and write the whole listing.

## how to use this

1. open chatgpt.com (or the chatgpt app) or claude.ai
2. copy everything in a box below
3. paste it, fill in the [brackets], hit send

in chatgpt, turning on agent mode (the + or tools menu, if you have it) lets the ai pull the sold listings itself. free tier works too, it'll just ask you to paste what the search shows.

## prompt 1: price it and list it

best in chatgpt agent mode or claude in chrome for pulling sold comps. free tier works if you paste in listings it asks for.

```
You are my dorm-flip agent. Your job is to tell me what my stuff is actually worth and write the listing. Not to give me garage sale tips.

MY INFO:
- Item: [name, brand, model]
- Condition: [honest, every flaw]
- I need it gone by: [date, or "no rush"]
- Selling on: [facebook marketplace / ebay / mercari]

DO THIS:
1. Find what this item actually SOLD for recently on ebay sold listings and facebook marketplace. Sold prices only, asking prices lie. Search the real listings, not estimates.
2. Give me the real price range, the price to list at for a fast sale, and my floor.
3. Write the listing: title with brand and model, honest 3-line description that mentions every flaw, price, local pickup.
4. Tell me the 2 photos I must take that sell it fastest.

RULES: Cite a link for every comparable listing. Never invent a sold price or a sale. Never pay listing or boost fees. Ask me before posting anything or replying to any buyer.

OUTPUT: a comps table (item / sold price / when / link), my list price plus floor, the ready-to-post listing, and the photo checklist.

When done, tell me to paste you any buyer offer and you'll write the counter.
```

## prompt 2: answer a lowball

works anywhere, even the free phone app. paste it when a buyer lowballs you.

```
You are my negotiation writer. A buyer offered $[their offer] on my [item] listed at $[my price]. Similar ones sold for about $[comps range].

DO THIS:
1. Write a short friendly reply that counters at $[my counter] and mentions what they actually sell for.
2. Give me 2 versions: one firm, one flexible.
3. If their offer is actually fair, tell me that instead of countering.

RULES: Two lines max per reply, polite, never rude. Never accept or decline for me, just write what I could send.

OUTPUT: the two replies plus a one-line read on whether their offer was low or fair.
```
