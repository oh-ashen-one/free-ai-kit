# calls: get your number off spam call lists

three moves, all free: the official list, your carrier's blocker, and the data brokers where spam callers buy your number.

## how to use this

1. open chatgpt.com (or the chatgpt app) or claude.ai
2. copy everything in the box below
3. paste it, fill in the [brackets], hit send

want the ai to walk slower or do the clicking? in chatgpt tap + or the tools menu and pick agent mode if you have it. on the free app it still works, it just walks you through the taps instead of doing them.

## the prompt

best in chatgpt agent mode or claude in chrome for the clicking parts. free tiers handle the walking-through parts fine.

```
You are my spam-call killer. Your job is to get my phone number off the lists spam callers use and turn on every free blocker I have. Not to explain why spam exists.

MY INFO:
- Carrier: [at&t / verizon / t-mobile / other]
- Phone: [iphone / android]
- How bad it is: [e.g. 5 calls a day, fake local numbers]

DO THIS:
1. Walk me through registering my number on donotcall.gov, step by step. It's free and takes 2 minutes.
2. Give me my carrier's free spam blocker and the exact taps to turn it on: at&t is activearmor (att.com/security/active-armor), verizon is call filter (verizon.com/support/call-filter-faqs), t-mobile is scam shield (t-mobile.com/benefits/scam-shield). If I said another carrier, find its free equivalent and link it.
3. Take me through the top data-broker opt-outs where spam callers buy numbers, one site at a time: suppression.peopleconnect.us, spokeo.com/optout, whitepages.com/suppression-requests, fastpeoplesearch.com/removal.
4. Show me how to report the worst numbers to the ftc at reportfraud.ftc.gov.

RULES: Real links only, cite each one. Never recommend a paid robocall app or paid removal service. Never type my number into a site that isn't an official opt-out page or my carrier. If a step needs a login or captcha, stop and tell me.

OUTPUT: a checklist table with columns: task / link / status done or skip / what it blocks.

When done, ask me if you should build my full data-broker opt-out plan, since that's where most spam starts.
```
