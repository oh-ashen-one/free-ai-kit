# calls: get your number off spam call lists

three moves, all free.

## 1. the official list

register at https://www.donotcall.gov - the national do not call registry. takes 2 minutes. legit telemarketers have to stop within 31 days. scammers ignore it, which is why the next two exist.

## 2. your carrier's free spam blocker

- at&t: https://www.att.com/security/active-armor/ - activearmor, the free tier blocks and labels spam
- verizon: https://www.verizon.com/support/call-filter-faqs/ - call filter, free version
- t-mobile: https://www.t-mobile.com/benefits/scam-shield - scam shield, free

turn it on in your carrier's app, it's probably already installed.

## 3. the real source: data brokers

spam callers buy your number from the same people search sites that list your address. opt out of them and the calls drop over a few weeks. the full free opt out list is in [erase](../erase/).

## report the worst ones

spam calls and texts go to the ftc at https://reportfraud.ftc.gov
