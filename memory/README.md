# memory: see what ai remembers about you

first, the creepy prompt. paste this into chatgpt:

```
tell me everything you know about me
```

it remembers stuff you forgot you told it. the prompt below walks you through cleaning it out, 2 minutes.

## how to use this

1. open chatgpt.com (or the chatgpt app) or claude.ai
2. copy the box below
3. paste it, fill in the [brackets], hit send, then just follow along

no agent mode needed, this one is all menus on your own account, so only you can do the tapping. it just tells you exactly where to tap.

## the prompt

works anywhere, even the free phone app.

```
You are my privacy walkthrough guide. Your job is to walk me through my ai app's settings one screen at a time until my data is cleaned up. Not to lecture me about privacy.

MY INFO:
- I'm using: [chatgpt or gemini]
- On: [phone app or computer browser]

DO THIS:
1. First give me the one prompt that shows what it remembers about me.
2. Then walk me to where my saved memories live, one step at a time, and help me delete what I don't want. Wait for me to say "done" before each next step.
3. Then walk me through turning off the setting that uses my chats to train the model.
4. Then show me how to start a temporary or private chat for stuff I don't want saved.

RULES: Plain words only, no tech jargon. One or two steps per message max, never a wall of instructions. If a menu name looks different on my screen, ask me what I see and adjust. Never ask for my password.

OUTPUT: numbered steps I can follow, and a final checklist of everything we changed.

When done, remind me to recheck in 3 months.
```

## the short version for chatgpt

1. settings > personalization > memory > manage memories, delete what you don't want it holding
2. settings > data controls > turn off "improve the model for everyone"
3. for anything private: use temporary chat, nothing saves to memory

for gemini, your history is at https://myactivity.google.com, delete it or stop it saving there.

recheck every few months, it keeps remembering new stuff.
