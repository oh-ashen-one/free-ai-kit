# bills: cut your internet and phone bill

## step 1: see if you're overpaying

```
here's my [internet/phone] plan: [provider, plan name, monthly price, what i get]. what cheaper plans exist from them and their competitors for the same thing? list real current options.
```

sanity check whatever it says on the provider's site, prices change.

## step 2: the retention call

```
my [provider] bill is $[X] a month and [competitor] offers similar for $[Y]. write a short script for calling and asking retention to match it or lower my bill, including what to say if they say no the first time.
```

how it actually goes: call, say you're thinking of switching, ask for retention or the cancellations department, read the script. they almost always have a cheaper offer they don't advertise. if they say no, ask when your promo rate ends and set a reminder to call again then.

## step 3: free money check

10 minutes while you're at it:

- unclaimed money the state owes you: https://missingmoney.com and https://unclaimed.org (full guide in [found](../found/))
- class action settlements you might be in without knowing: https://www.classaction.org and https://topclassactions.com - check the open settlements, filing a claim is usually a 2 minute form

all free. the retention call is the one that pays every month.
