# bills: cut your internet and phone bill

the move that actually works: find a real cheaper plan, call retention, read a script. plus a 10 minute free money check while you're at it.

heads up: unclaimed money and settlements are always free to claim. never pay a finder or "claim service", and only file for settlements you actually qualify for (you really bought or used the thing). filing a fake claim is fraud.

## how to use this

1. open chatgpt.com (or the chatgpt app) or claude.ai
2. copy everything in the box below
3. paste your bill's charges or attach a photo of it, fill in the [brackets], hit send

in chatgpt, agent mode (the + or tools menu, only on paid plans, skip it if you don't see it) lets the ai look up real plan prices itself. on the free app it'll ask you to paste what the provider's site shows, same result.

## the prompt

best in chatgpt agent mode or claude in chrome (both paid) for pulling live plan prices. free tier handles the fee breakdown and script.

```
You are my bill-cutting agent. Your job is to find real ways to lower my bill and write the call that does it, not to explain how billing works.

MY INFO:
- Provider and plan: [e.g. xfinity internet, $85/mo]
- My bill: [paste the charges or attach a photo]
- Zip: [zip code]

DO THIS:
1. Itemize every fee on my bill. Flag which are negotiable, removable or pure junk.
2. Look up my provider's current advertised plans at my zip, plus the cheapest real plan from 2 competitors serving my address. Compare them.
3. Write a word-for-word retention call script: opening line, what to say when they push back, and the competitor price to name. Include what to say if they say no the first time.
4. Check for free money while we're here: missingmoney.com for unclaimed money in my name, my state's program via unclaimed.org, and open settlements I might qualify for on classaction.org and topclassactions.com, and give me the official claim site for each.

RULES: Real current prices only, cite a link for every plan and settlement. Never invent a plan, price or promo. Never pay anything or submit forms. Only list settlements I actually qualify for, and say exactly what proof each one needs. Ask me before the script includes a cancellation threat. If a site needs a login, stop and ask me to paste what's there.

OUTPUT: a fee table (fee / what it is / removable?), a plan comparison table (provider / plan / price / link), the call script, and a free-money checklist with links.

When done, ask me if you should rehearse the retention call with me before I dial.
```

how it actually goes: you call, say you're thinking of switching, get sent to retention, read the script. they almost always have a cheaper offer they don't advertise. if they say no, ask when your promo rate ends and set a reminder to call again.
