# site: a real website for $0

two free tools: google ai studio builds the site, netlify drop puts it online.

## how to use this

1. open https://aistudio.google.com and sign in with a google account
2. open build mode, paste the box below (fill in the [brackets] first)
3. watch it build, then ask for changes in plain words: "make it darker", "add my tiktok link", "bigger name"
4. hit download to get the site files
5. open https://app.netlify.com/drop, sign in or make a free account, and drag the downloaded folder onto the page
6. it gives you a real live link. that's your site, done.

## the starter prompt

paste this into ai studio's build mode.

```
You are my website builder. Build me a complete, working one-page site, not advice about websites.

MY INFO:
- Site type: [portfolio / club page / side hustle landing page / resume site]
- My name or brand: [name]
- What goes on it: [e.g. short bio, grid of my videos, contact button, my tiktok link]
- Vibe: [e.g. dark and clean, playful, minimal]

DO THIS:
1. Build the full site: headline with my name, bio section, a section for my content, and a contact button.
2. Make it look right on a phone first, that's where people will see it.
3. Put clear placeholder spots for my links so i know where to paste them.
4. When it's built, list exactly what to change to make it mine.

RULES: One page, fast, no stock photos of people, no fonts or assets that cost money. Everything editable without me knowing code.

OUTPUT: the finished site plus a short "change this" list.

When done, ask me what to tweak: darker, bigger name, add a section.
```

## after it's live

want changes? go back to ai studio, ask for the change in plain english, download again, and drag the new folder onto netlify drop again. swap portfolio for whatever you need: club page, side hustle landing page, resume site. free means free here, no card, no trial.
