# codes: my photo style prompts

one pic becomes a whole photoshoot. upload your photo to chatgpt or gemini, paste a style.

heads up: these are just saved prompts, not secret commands. every one already includes "keep my face and outfit exactly the same" because without it the ai changes your fit.

## paparazzi

```
turn this into a candid paparazzi street photo: camera flash at night, slight motion blur, caught mid-step on a city sidewalk. keep my face and outfit exactly the same.
```

## film35

```
turn this into a 35mm film photo: visible grain, warm faded tones, slight light leak on one edge, shot on a point and shoot. keep my face and outfit exactly the same.
```
