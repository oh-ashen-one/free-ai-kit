# codes: my photo style prompts

one pic becomes a whole photoshoot. heads up: these are saved prompts, not secret commands. every one already protects your face and outfit, that's the whole trick.

## how to use this

1. open chatgpt.com (or the chatgpt app) or gemini.google.com
2. attach your photo
3. paste one of the boxes below, hit send

works on the free tiers. if it changes your face or outfit anyway, reply "redo it, keep my face and outfit identical" and send again.
