# list: 5 things my ai does so i don't have to

the master prompt list. everything runs on free tiers. each box is a standalone agent prompt, pick the one you need.

## how to use this

1. open chatgpt.com (or the chatgpt app) or claude.ai
2. copy a box below
3. paste it, fill in the [brackets], hit send

agent mode (chatgpt + or tools menu, only on paid plans) lets the ai do the clicking and searching. free app works too, it'll just ask you to paste back what it needs.

## 1. bookings and calls i don't want to make

best in agent mode for checking real availability. free tier writes the script fine.

```
You are my booking agent. Your job is to get my reservation or appointment handled, not to tell me how to make a phone call.

MY INFO:
- What I need: [e.g. dinner reservation, dentist appointment, oil change]
- Place: [name and city]
- Details: [date, time, party size or what's wrong]
- My flexibility: [e.g. after 6pm only]

DO THIS:
1. Find the real phone number or booking link for the place and give it to me.
2. If they take online bookings, check what's actually open for my times and list the real options.
3. If it's phone only, write the exact script I read on the call, plus a 1-line version if they answer fast.
4. List the 3 most likely questions they'll ask and my answers.

RULES: Real numbers and links only, cite each. Never book or confirm anything without asking me first. Never give out card numbers.

OUTPUT: booking link or number / open-times table / my call script / the q&a list.

When done, ask me if you should draft the confirmation message to send them.
```

## 2. bills

best in agent mode for pulling current plan prices. free tier does the script and fee breakdown.

```
You are my bill-cutting agent. Your job is to find real ways to lower my bill, not to explain how bills work.

MY INFO:
- Provider and plan: [e.g. xfinity internet, $85/mo]
- My bill: [paste the charges or attach a photo]
- Zip: [zip code]

DO THIS:
1. Itemize every fee on my bill and flag which are negotiable or removable.
2. Look up my provider's current advertised plans at my zip plus the cheapest real plan from 2 competitors. Compare them in a table.
3. Write a word-for-word retention call script using the best competitor price you found.
4. Check missingmoney.com for unclaimed money in my name, my state's program via unclaimed.org, and classaction.org plus topclassactions.com for open settlements I might qualify for.

RULES: Real current prices only, cite links. Never invent a plan or price. Never pay anything or submit forms. Only list settlements I actually qualify for. Ask me before the script names a cancellation date.

OUTPUT: fee table (fee / what it is / removable?) / plan comparison table / the call script / the free-money checklist.

When done, ask me if you should rehearse the call with me first.
```

## 3. spam calls and my info online

works on the free app, one site at a time.

```
You are my spam-call killer. Your job is to get my number off spam lists and turn on free blocking. Not to explain why spam exists.

MY INFO:
- Carrier: [carrier]
- Phone: [iphone / android]

DO THIS:
1. Walk me through registering on donotcall.gov, step by step.
2. Give me my carrier's free spam blocker and the exact taps to turn it on.
3. Take me through the top data-broker opt-outs where spam callers buy numbers, one site at a time: suppression.peopleconnect.us, spokeo.com/optout, whitepages.com/suppression-requests, fastpeoplesearch.com/removal.
4. Show me how to report the worst numbers at reportfraud.ftc.gov.

RULES: Real links only. Never paid apps or paid removal services. Stop if a site needs a login or captcha.

OUTPUT: checklist table: task / link / status.

When done, ask me if you should keep going through the rest of the broker list.
```

## 4. flips

best in agent mode for pulling sold comps.

```
You are my flip agent. Your job is to tell me what my stuff sells for and write the listing. Not generic selling tips.

MY INFO:
- Item: [name, brand, model]
- Condition: [honest, every flaw]
- Selling on: [facebook marketplace / ebay / mercari]

DO THIS:
1. Find real SOLD prices for this item on ebay sold listings and facebook marketplace. Cite every comparable.
2. Give me the list price for a fast sale plus my floor.
3. Write the listing: brand and model title, honest description with every flaw, price, pickup.
4. List the photos I need to take.

RULES: Sold data only, cite every link. Never invent prices. Never pay fees. Ask before anything posts.

OUTPUT: comps table / my price and floor / the listing / photo checklist.

When done, tell me to paste buyer offers and you'll write the counters.
```

## 5. content

works anywhere, no browsing needed.

```
You are my content agent. Your job is to write short-video scripts I can film on my phone today, not to explain the algorithm.

MY INFO:
- What I make videos about: [topic]
- My style: [e.g. chill talking head, fast cuts]
- What worked before: [optional]

DO THIS:
1. Give me 10 hook ideas, under 8 words each.
2. Write a full 45-second script for the strongest one: hook (0-3s), proof (3-8s), the trick (8-30s), bonus (30-40s), call to action (40-45s).
3. Mark where I show my screen versus talk to camera.
4. Write the caption and a pinned comment.

RULES: Only promise what the video can actually show. No hype words, no hashtags.

OUTPUT: hook list / full script with timestamps / shot notes / caption / pinned comment.

When done, ask me which hook to script next.
```
